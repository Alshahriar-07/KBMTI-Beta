# Market structure

Higher highs (HH) and higher lows (HL) describe an uptrend. Lower highs (LH) and lower
lows (LL) describe a downtrend. A range contains overlapping swings without a clean
sequence.

A break of structure (BOS) confirms continuation when price closes beyond the most
recent swing in the direction of the prevailing trend. A change of character (CHoCH)
is the first close beyond the swing that defined the prior leg, against that trend. A
CHoCH is a warning of transition, not proof of reversal.

Confirmation must use closed candles. A wick through a swing is not a confirmed break
unless the strategy explicitly says so.

Support and resistance are zones where prior reactions clustered. They are evidence,
not guarantees. A level with more independent touches is more visible to the market,
but touch count alone does not predict a reaction.

Trend continuation means the higher-timeframe sequence remains intact and a pullback
holds above the last higher low (or below the last lower high). Trend reversal requires
a confirmed structural break and subsequent maintenance of the new sequence.
