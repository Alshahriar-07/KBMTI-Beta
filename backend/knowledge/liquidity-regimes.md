# Liquidity, breakouts and regimes

A liquidity sweep is a wick beyond a visible high or low followed by a close back
inside the prior range. KBMTI labels these as candidates, not proof that a specific
participant was stopped out.

A false breakout is a close beyond a level that fails to hold and returns inside the
range. A breakout-retest setup waits for the level to be reclaimed as support or
resistance before confirmation.

Supply and demand zones are areas of prior imbalance or strong departure. They are
reference areas, not exact prices.

Market regimes are descriptive: trending up, trending down, ranging, high volatility
or uncertain. A regime is determined from deterministic features. Conflicting evidence
must produce uncertain rather than a forced label.

Trend following prefers aligned structure and momentum. Pullback entries wait for a
retest of a reference area with a closed-bar confirmation. Momentum continuation buys
or sells in the direction of an established impulse, but a late entry after an extended
move has worse location even if the trend is intact.
